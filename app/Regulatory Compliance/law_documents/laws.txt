🧠 1. 2026 Amendment to the IT Rules — AI & Synthetic Content Regulation

India has amended its main digital law — the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules — to explicitly cover AI-generated content:

Key features:
-Defines and regulates Synthetic Generated Information (SGI) — AI-generated/altered text, images, audio, video that appears real.
-Platforms must comply with faster timelines for takedowns of harmful/high-risk AI content.
-Requires labeling/watermarking for AI-generated content shared online.
-Regulators can enforce takedowns within hours (instead of days) for deepfakes, impersonation, synthetic NCII (non-consensual imagery), and misinformation.
-Affects digital intermediaries (platforms, social media, hosting services), with real-time removal and accountability requirements.

📍 Impact: This is India’s most significant AI-related legal intervention implemented so far, regulating where AI intersects with content distribution, misinformation, and harm.

📜 2. AI Ethics & Accountability Legislative Proposal (Private Member Bill)

A parliamentary proposal titled the Artificial Intelligence (Ethics & Accountability) Bill, 2025 has been introduced to create more structured AI governance:

Key Elements Suggested in the Bill:
-Framework for ethical use of AI in India.
-Proposed penalties up to ₹5 crore for misuse or violations.
-Sets up potential oversight mechanisms and an independent ethics committee to monitor AI systems.

📌 Note: As of early 2026, this is still a parliamentary proposal and not yet codified into law like the amended IT Rules.

📊 3. IndiaAI Mission / Policy Initiatives

While not strictly “laws,” India’s national mission and strategy platforms play a regulatory influence and shape AI governance priorities:

🇮🇳 IndiaAI Mission

-Approved by the Cabinet with significant funding.
-Drives national AI ecosystem growth — including ethics, inclusion, “responsible AI,” and public-good objectives.
-Hosts the India AI Impact Summit, a platform for policy proposals and global alignment.

AI Impact Summit Initiatives

-Focus on People, Planet & Progress principles.
-Promotes awareness, responsible AI adoption, and citizen participation.

📁 4. Existing Related Laws with AI Implications

India does not yet regulate AI through a dedicated, standalone “AI Act,” but several existing laws apply to AI-related issues:

🛡️ Information Technology Act, 2000

-Regulates digital intermediaries, cybersecurity, and unlawful digital content; now amended to include AI content provisions.

📊 Digital Personal Data Protection Rules

-Regulate personal data collection and processing. AI systems that handle personal data must comply with these privacy norms.

⚖️ Other sectoral laws

-Consumer protection, cybercrime statutes, and child protection laws (e.g., POCSO) are often invoked where AI misuse overlaps with such harms.

🧭 5. Drafts / Future AI Governance Debates (Policy Vision)

There’s ongoing discussion in Indian policy circles about broader AI governance frameworks, including:
-Establishing independent AI oversight bodies.
-AI risk classification and safety standards. (referenced in government & think-tank research)
-Incorporating AI ethics into laws beyond digital content.

These discussions may someday inform a more comprehensive AI regulatory law akin to the EU’s AI Act, but as of March 2026 they are still in development.
